Highest-leverage fixes: (a) bump the apply model (one line in api-cost-config.yaml) to a frontier model — it's one call per application, cost trivial; (b) add a `role_archetype_map` (headline + lead_pillar per archetype) + `source_bullets` (pillar-keyed, "select don't rewrite") to profile.yaml — i.e., encode our positioning thesis + master-CV bullets as config; (c) raise truncation to ~6000 chars / token-budget; (d) use the existing `--notes` flag for per-role hints today (zero code).
