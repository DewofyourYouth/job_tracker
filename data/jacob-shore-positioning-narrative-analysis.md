Your moat is a combination almost nobody else can claim:

> **"I ship and own production systems in the hardest deployment environments that exist — air-gapped, sovereign, regulated, no-hotfix-over-the-internet — I talk to the customer directly with no PM buffer, and I build the AI/agentic layer on top."**

Every clause is evidenced:

- **Customer-facing, no PM** — ran technical reviews directly with foreign-government security teams; translated analyst requirements into architecture.

That combination is exactly what a specific, *less-saturated* set of roles is desperate for.

## The three proof pillars to lead with

Everywhere — CV, LinkedIn, cover letters, interview opener — lead with these three, reordering by role:

1. **Air-gapped / B2G deployment at sovereign scale** — the rare one. Few engineers have shipped where a bad deploy can't be hotfixed over the internet.
2. **AI/agentic systems you've actually built and iterated** — kills the Mylo objection. Back it with your public blog posts as proof-of-work; very few candidates can show their engineering reasoning in public.
3. **Customer-facing technical ownership with no PM layer** — the Forward-Deployed/Solutions signal.

Rule of thumb: pick the pillar the specific employer cares about most and open with it. For an AI startup, lead with #2. For a defense/security/fintech infra company, lead with #1. For an FDE/solutions role, lead with #3.
